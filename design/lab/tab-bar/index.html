Fixed from v1

  • Every tab and button is at least 44 pixels: v1's laptop tabs, bell and close button were 34 to 40.
  • The right end of the bar's blue is deeper, and the lit tab's white wash a little lighter, so every word on the bar passes AA. v1's words there fell to about 4 to 1.
  • The menu's section names are darker grey: v1's light grey was 2.6 to 1.
  • One plain colour for every icon tile, and for the head's square and role chip. v1 gave each row its own colour, and each role one (red for an owner); in v2 a colour means a status.
  • Sign out is a plain row, not red: signing out is not a danger. Its word is Sign out, not Logout (docs/GLOSSARY.md).
  • The owner's home tab and chip say Owner, not Admin: one name per thing.
  • On a laptop the bar spans the page's frame, 1,200 pixels, so its outer tabs sit above the content's edges, as v1's did over its 800-pixel frame.
  • Left out for now: the lit tab gliding to the next one on a phone, and the faint mark on a tapped tab while its page loads (polish, after go-live).
