# Design decisions

What the UI lab chose, one line each, with its picture. How the lab works:
`CONTRIBUTING.md`. The rules for every screen: `docs/DESIGN.md`.

| Decision | Why | Picture |
|---|---|---|
| Base look | Chosen from three designs (a proposed one, v1 as it is, a premium one) plus a mix of their best parts: a quiet page, a deeper blue, colour only for status, and from v1 the status edge, the big amount and the row of dates. Every text colour passes AA. | [base-look.jpg](base-look.jpg) |
| Live board | Chosen from two designs (v1 rebuilt, and a premium refined v1): v1's car card and contents, because the owners settled each part and the staff already know it. The paint stripe and colour stay exactly as v1. Fixed to our rules: 1, 2 or 3 columns, "On hold" in words, AA contrast, no inline style. From the refined design, the lighter View drawer, with v1's working clock. | [live-board.jpg](live-board.jpg) |
| Job card | Chosen from two designs (v1 rebuilt, and v1 reworked with the work first): v1's job card as it is, because the staff know it and it keeps the screen calm. The parts tables still scroll sideways on a phone; three ways to stop that were tried and felt cluttered. Kept from v1: the row number pinned on the left and the row being edited lit up. Added: while a row is edited, a tag above it gives its number and full name, so a price never goes into the wrong part. No Add buttons: each list keeps one open, empty row at its end, and typing into it opens the next (v1's Old Bills); an untouched open row is never saved, and its status stays grey. No hint text in empty boxes but the stock search's. On a laptop, the customer's name, number and note share one row. The plate comes first, before Brand and Model, as on v1's Old Bills, so a known car fills them. The VIN has a small meter, 17 bars and a count, that turns green as it is typed and red past 17. Fixed to our rules: every box 44px, status colours that pass AA, box edges at 3 to 1, a name for every box, and a box with the keyboard focus never hidden under the pinned header. | [job-card.jpg](job-card.jpg) |
| Owner dashboard | Chosen from two designs (v1 rebuilt, and v1 made better): v1's Profit page (Analysis & Reports, owners only), with three parts of the refined design. The cash card shows Money in and Money out as a green and a red panel. The profit for each month is a line: green above zero, red below, the latest month on the right scale, and a tap on a month gives its figure. There is no zoom, because a year is 12 points and the period buttons choose the range. One card under the equation, "Before you trust these figures", holds every warning, each with where, why and how. A Fix is a definite error and goes only when fixed. A Check is a guess from an entry's name and goes when fixed or marked as checked. Hide folds the card. The current month's wages are never a warning: they are missing every month until the month is settled, so a Not final chip sits on the profit itself and cannot be hidden. On a laptop the boxes sit in two columns that end level, in the same frame as every screen. Fixed to our rules: no amount owed is green, no icon on every row, no inline style or script, every control 44px, AA contrast. | [owner-dashboard.jpg](owner-dashboard.jpg) |
| Sign-in | v1's sign-in page rebuilt, in the two steps of ADR-0025: first an email or a username, then the code (an owner) or the password (Office and Floor). Kept from v1: the name above one centred card, tall boxes and button, the round button under the card, and "Authorised access only". Fixed to our rules: the product's name, not one workshop's, because the page cannot know the workshop yet; a blue button with no red line and no grey shading, because colour means status; the shared boxes and buttons; no hint text in empty boxes; 12-pixel corners; AA contrast and 44-pixel taps. | [sign-in.jpg](sign-in.jpg) |
